About Me

I am a PhD candidate in astrophysics researching interstellar dust and dust grain models.

My research focuses on understanding the physical properties of interstellar dust by combining dust grain models with observational constraints.

Research

My research focuses on interstellar dust grain models, including grain composition, size distributions, and the connection between theoretical models and astrophysical observations.

GrainBow

I am developing GrainBow, an open-source dust model framework and Bayesian fitting tool for studying interstellar dust grain populations.
